Can a phone read the pane's code?

The real code, autonomous.ai/app, drawn the size it would be in a pane's title bar, on the right before the agent and model, in the pane's own colours with no background. Open this page on the Mac at 100% zoom, and point the iPhone Camera at each one. Note the smallest size where the yellow link appears quickly, in each mode.

  1. From where you'd naturally hold the phone to scan (about 30 cm).
  2. Then from 10–15 cm.
  3. For 14.5 px, the proposed size, also as close as the phone will still focus.

In dark mode the code is inverted (light squares on dark). The QR standard allows that, but not every scanner reads it. The square rows show whether the rounded corners cost any reading.
